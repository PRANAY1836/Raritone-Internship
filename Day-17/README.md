# AI/ML Internship — Day 17

## 3D Avatar Generation & Body Reconstruction Research

### Objective

Study the role of 3D avatar generation and human body reconstruction in Virtual Try-On systems. Analyze how digital avatars are created, reconstructed, and utilized to improve garment fitting and personalized shopping experiences.

---

## Topics Covered

- 3D Avatar Generation
- Human Body Reconstruction
- 3D Mesh Creation
- Texture Mapping
- Digital Human Modeling
- Virtual Try-On Integration

- 3D avatars improve garment fitting accuracy.
- Body reconstruction enhances personalization.
- Realistic avatars provide better user experiences.
- Accurate body models support advanced cloth simulation.
- Digital humans play a key role in future fashion technologies.
