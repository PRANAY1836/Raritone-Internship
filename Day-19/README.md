## Project Overview

This repository contains the complete work completed during the AI/ML Internship at Raritone. The internship focused on researching and understanding the Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision technologies used in modern fashion applications, particularly AI-Based Virtual Try-On and Fashion Recommendation Systems.

The work involved studying body segmentation, pose estimation, body landmark detection, body measurement extraction, garment warping, 2D and 3D Virtual Try-On models, fashion datasets, AI recommendation systems, and complete workflow design.
