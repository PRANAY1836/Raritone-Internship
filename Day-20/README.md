## Overview

This repository contains the research work completed for the final internship task, focusing on understanding how multiple technical teams collaborate to build an AI-powered fashion platform.

The study explores the interaction between Artificial Intelligence, Backend Engineering, and Data Analytics, highlighting how these domains work together to deliver intelligent Virtual Try-On and personalized recommendation services.

---

## Research Objectives

- Understand the overall project architecture.
- Analyze the responsibilities of different technical teams.
- Study the interaction between AI/ML, Backend, and Data Analytics.
- Explore the complete business workflow.
- Understand how cross-functional collaboration improves the platform.
