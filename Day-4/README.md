# AI/ML Internship — Day 4

Here you will find the list of tasks completed on Day 4 of the AI/ML Internship. The tasks focused on improving Human Pose Detection systems, studying Virtual Try-On AI Models, testing Body Segmentation methods, developing Clothing Recommendation Logic, and comparing MediaPipe with OpenPose using Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision techniques.

---

# Tasks Completed

## 1. Improved Pose Detection Accuracy

Enhanced the real-time pose detection system using:
- MediaPipe
- OpenCV
- Python

Features include:
- Real-time body landmark detection
- Skeleton tracking
- Improved pose accuracy
- Webcam-based AI pose estimation
- Stable body tracking

The system was improved by increasing detection confidence, improving landmark stability, and reducing tracking flickering.

---

## 2. Research on Virtual Try-On AI Models

Conducted detailed research on:
- AI virtual fitting systems
- Digital clothing simulation
- AI fashion technology
- Body tracking for virtual try-on systems

In this research, different AI techniques used for virtual clothing fitting and body alignment were studied along with their applications in online fashion platforms.

---

## 3. Body Segmentation Method Testing

Studied and tested AI body segmentation systems used in computer vision applications.

Topics covered:
- Human body segmentation
- AI-based body masking
- Foreground extraction
- Segmentation workflows

Applications include:
- Virtual try-on systems
- AI fashion platforms
- Human body analysis
- Background removal systems

---

## 4. Clothing Recommendation Logic

Researched AI-based clothing recommendation systems used in online fashion platforms.

Concepts studied:
- Personalized fashion recommendation
- Machine learning recommendation systems
- Customer preference prediction
- AI shopping assistance

The task explored how AI systems analyze customer behavior and body measurements to provide intelligent fashion recommendations.

---

## 5. MediaPipe vs OpenPose Comparison

Performed a detailed comparison between:
- MediaPipe
- OpenPose

Comparison areas:
- Accuracy
- Speed
- Real-time performance
- Hardware requirements
- Ease of implementation
- AI workflow efficiency

The comparison showed that MediaPipe performs efficiently for lightweight real-time applications, while OpenPose provides better multi-person tracking and advanced pose estimation accuracy.
