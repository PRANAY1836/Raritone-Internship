# AI/ML Internship — Day 9

## Data Intern

### Research on Fashion Datasets and Data Preparation for Virtual Try-On Systems

---

## Objective

Study the importance of fashion datasets and understand the processes involved in dataset collection, cleaning, metadata generation, annotation, and testing sample preparation for AI-based Virtual Try-On applications.

---

## Research Areas

### Datasets Studied
- DeepFashion2
- VITON-HD

### Data Preparation
- Dataset Collection
- Data Cleaning
- Dataset Organization
- Testing Sample Preparation

### Annotation & Metadata
- Bounding Box Annotation
- Landmark Annotation
- Segmentation Masks
- Clothing Attributes
- Metadata Generation

---

## Applications

- Virtual Try-On Systems
- Fashion Recommendation Engines
- Clothing Recognition
- Smart Retail
- AI Fashion Research

---

## Key Learnings

- Importance of high-quality datasets.
- Role of metadata in dataset management.
- Annotation techniques used in fashion AI.
- Data preparation workflow for machine learning models.
- Dataset organization for efficient AI training.

This research explored the role of fashion datasets in Artificial Intelligence applications.
