## About the Internship

This repository contains all the research, documentation, implementations, and final deliverables completed during the **Raritone AI/ML Summer Internship 2026**.

The internship focused on understanding the role of **Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Machine Learning** in building modern fashion technology applications, particularly **AI-Based Virtual Try-On Systems** and **Fashion Recommendation Engines**.

Throughout the internship, multiple AI architectures, datasets, workflows, and emerging technologies were explored through structured research and practical demonstrations.

## Internship Objectives

* Understand AI applications in fashion technology.
* Study Computer Vision fundamentals.

* Research body landmark detection and measurement extraction.
* Analyze Virtual Try-On architectures.
* Study garment warping techniques.
* Explore 3D avatar generation technologies.
